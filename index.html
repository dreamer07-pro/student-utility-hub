💡 Student Tips

Practical tips to help you succeed in college and school.

📖 Study Habits
Study in focused blocks of 25–50 minutes with short breaks. Active recall and spaced repetition beat re-reading notes every time.
📝 Exam Preparation
Start revising at least two weeks before exams. Solve previous year papers and mock tests under timed conditions.
⏰ Time Management
Plan your day the night before. Use a single to-do list and tackle the hardest task first thing in the morning.
💻 Programming Practice
Code a little every day. Build small projects instead of only watching tutorials. Read other people's code on GitHub.
📄 Resume Preparation
Keep it one page. Quantify achievements (e.g., "Improved load time by 30%"). Tailor it for every role you apply to.
🎤 Interview Preparation
Practice explaining your projects out loud. Prepare STAR-format answers (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioral questions.
😴 Sleep and Rest
Aim for 7–8 hours of sleep. A rested brain remembers more and learns faster than one fueled by caffeine and all-nighters.
🚫 Avoiding Procrastination
Use the 2-minute rule: if a task takes less than 2 minutes, do it now. Break big tasks into tiny, less scary steps.
